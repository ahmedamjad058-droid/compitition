Who We Are

Ocean Legacy Miniatures started in Sialkot with a small workshop and a big dream - to bring back the golden age of sailing ships. We don't have a big factory, we have skilled hands.

Each miniature is handcrafted from old teak, rosewood and uses antique brass. We research original blueprints before we build.
